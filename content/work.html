Financial platform

MuniHub

Platform: WordPress

The need

The municipal market needed one place to follow new bond issues and the tools around them.

What we built

A WordPress platform that brings together a new issue calendar, deal spotlights with sale dates and amounts, digital roadshows and AI tools for the municipal market, with member sign in for deal teams.

AI search tool
