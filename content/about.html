About Saxon Enterprises

Every website is a voyage. We help you plot the course.

Look closely at our logo and you will find a sail tucked inside the O of Saxon. It is there on purpose. A good website, like a good voyage, needs a clear destination, a sound vessel and someone keeping watch long after it leaves the harbor. That is the work we do, and it is why our tagline is Navigating Your Ideas.

How we sail

Chart the course

Before anything is designed we ask what the site has to do: sell a canvas, book a band, move a bond deal, answer a buyer. Every page earns its place against that goal.

Build a sound hull

We write our own lean themes and plugins instead of stacking page builders and add ons. Fewer moving parts means faster pages, fewer updates and far fewer ways in for attackers.

Keep a weather eye

Launch day is the start, not the finish. We back up before every change, apply updates, watch for trouble and keep a written log of what changed and why.

Make port where buyers look

People now ask search engines and AI assistants who to hire. We structure every site so those tools can understand it, quote it and send customers your way.

Who we are

Saxon Enterprises, Inc. is a small studio led by William Leonard. We design, build and manage websites and web apps for an unusually wide crew: an AI search visibility tool, a municipal bond platform and its digital roadshows, an identity governance product, a horizontal drilling contractor, an online shop for authenticated religious art and a pipe and drum band that has played parades since 1993.

Those projects have almost nothing in common, and that is the point. We are not a template shop. We listen first, then build exactly what the job calls for, whether that is a five page brochure, a members area, an online store or a secure workflow with approvals and analytics.

What you can expect from us

  • Straight answers. A clear scope, a clear price and plain English, before any work begins.
  • Your site, your keys. You own the domain, the content and the code. Everything we build is kept in version control and documented.
  • Security by default. Modern sign in with Google or Microsoft, spam protection we wrote ourselves, and email that goes through your own business account.
  • Accessible and fast. Readable contrast, keyboard friendly menus and pages that load quickly on a phone.
  • One crew, start to finish. The people who build your site are the people who look after it.
