For twenty years, being found online meant ranking on a page of blue links. That is changing fast. Buyers now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI answers who they should hire, and those tools reply with a short list of names. Answer engine optimization, or AEO, is the work of making sure your name is on that list.

What answer engines look for

AI tools favor sources they can understand and trust. In practice that means:

  • Clear facts in plain text. Who you are, what you do, where you work and for whom, stated directly rather than hidden in images or vague slogans.
  • Structured data. Schema markup that labels your business, services, pages and articles so machines do not have to guess.
  • Real answers to real questions. Pages that answer the questions buyers actually ask, in their words.
  • Consistency. The same name, address and services everywhere your business appears online.
  • Speed and accessibility. Fast, well structured pages are easier for every kind of crawler to read.
